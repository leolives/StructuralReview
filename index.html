PE Civil: Structural — Topic Map + Practice Examples

80 questions · 9 hours · SI + USCS

1) Analysis of Structures — Loads and Load Applications

12–18 questions
  • Dead, live, construction, wind, seismic, moving, snow/rain/ice, and impact loads
  • Earth pressure + surcharge loads
  • Tributary areas and load paths (vertical/lateral)
  • Load combinations

2) Analysis of Structures — Forces and Load Effects

17–26 questions
  • Shear and moment diagrams
  • Axial tension/compression, shear, flexure, and combined stresses
  • Deflection checks
  • Special topics: torsion, buckling, fatigue, bearing, thermal effects

3) Temporary Structures and Other Topics

5–8 questions
  • Special inspections and submittals
  • Formwork, falsework, shoring/reshoring, scaffolding, bracing, anchorage
  • Impact of construction on adjacent facilities
  • Safety topics (construction/roadside/work zone)

4) Design & Details — Materials and Material Properties

10–15 questions
  • Soil classification and soil properties
  • Concrete, steel, timber, masonry behavior and use
  • Material test methods and specification conformance

5) Design & Details — Component Design and Detailing

26–39 questions
  • Horizontal members (beams, slabs, diaphragms, struts)
  • Vertical members (columns, bearing walls, shear walls)
  • Systems (trusses, braces, frames, composite)
  • Connections (bolted, welded, anchors, embedments)
  • Shallow and deep foundations + retaining walls

Practice Example Questions

I included a starter set below so your page has direct practice aligned to each NCEES topic area.

Q1 — Load combinations (Sec. 1)

A steel beam supports dead, live, and roof snow loads. Determine the controlling ASCE 7 LRFD load combination and factored demand.

Q2 — Seismic base shear path (Sec. 1 + 2)

Given a 3-story shear wall building with diaphragm reactions, compute the story shear distribution and overturning moment at the base.

Q3 — Shear/moment + deflection (Sec. 2)

For a simply supported beam with uniform and point loads, draw V-M diagrams and find maximum deflection using EI.

Q4 — Temporary bracing check (Sec. 3)

A formwork panel is laterally braced during concrete placement. Check brace force and anchorage adequacy under construction loads.

Q5 — Material properties (Sec. 4)

Given index properties and SPT data, classify soil and estimate bearing behavior impacts for footing serviceability checks.

Q6 — RC flexural design (Sec. 5)

Design tension steel for a rectangular reinforced concrete beam section under factored moment per ACI 318 and verify strain limits.
